About Us

A local team of builders passionate about creating quality homes for Waikato families.

Our Story

Built on quality, honesty and hard work

Waikato Building Solutions was founded with a simple goal: to give homeowners a building experience they can genuinely feel good about. Too many people had told us stories of blown budgets, missed deadlines and builders who stopped answering the phone. We set out to do things differently.

Today, our team of qualified builders and trusted subcontractors deliver new homes, renovations and extensions throughout Hamilton, Cambridge, Te Awamutu and the wider Waikato. Every project — big or small — gets the same attention to detail and the same commitment to doing the job right.

We're proud that most of our work comes from referrals and repeat clients. That's the best measure of a builder we know.
