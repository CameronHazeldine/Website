About Us

Owner-operated by Licensed Building Practitioner Cameron Hazeldine, building quality homes for Waikato families.

Our Story

Built on quality, honesty and hard work

Waikato Building Solutions is owned and run by Cameron Hazeldine. Cameron grew up on a farm in Piopio, where he learned early that a job worth doing is worth doing properly, and that hard work and honesty go a long way.

He has brought those same values to residential building for over 10 years. As a Licensed Building Practitioner (LBP), Cameron delivers new homes, renovations and extensions throughout Hamilton, Cambridge, Te Awamutu and the wider Waikato.

Because the business is owner-operated, you deal directly with Cameron from your first conversation to the day you get the keys. Every project, big or small, gets the same attention to detail and the same commitment to doing the job right.
