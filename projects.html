Bach Renovation, Raglan

📍 Bay View Road, RaglanComplete renovation

A complete renovation of an original Raglan bach overlooking the harbour. We brought the bach up to modern standards while keeping its character, and deliberately left one side original, with its weathered weatherboards, as a nod to its history.

A standout feature is the custom-made window benchtop, crafted from timber supplied off the client's own farm.

  • Complete renovation of an original bach
  • One side left original to preserve its character
  • Custom window benchtop made from the client's own farm timber
